The knowledge garage

Practical frameworks for buying, maintaining and preserving a decades-old vehicle. Model-specific procedures require verification before use.

01

Before you buy

Inspect body condition, documentation, service records, cold start, cooling, transmission, suspension, brakes and electrical functions. Arrange a specialist inspection.

Inspection framework ↗
02

Preventive maintenance

Build a baseline after purchase: fluids, filters, belts, hoses, tyres, brake condition and age-sensitive rubber parts—using vehicle-specific service data.

Maintenance reference ↗
03

Cooling & A/C

Hot weather increases the importance of a healthy cooling system. Avoid guessing: confirm temperature readings, fan operation and refrigerant-system diagnosis with qualified help.

Source standards ↗
04

Electrical diagnosis

Document symptoms, battery and charging condition, fuses and diagnostic codes where supported. Safety-critical systems need suitable equipment and expertise.

Diagnostic approach ↗
05

Restoration planning

Prioritise structural condition, mechanical safety, originality, parts availability and a realistic contingency budget before cosmetic work.

Stories & gallery ↗
06

Parts verification

Match by chassis, engine, production date and part number using reliable catalogues. Similar appearance does not prove compatibility.

Resource directory ↗
Safety: this site is educational. It is not a workshop manual. Do not work under an unsupported vehicle, open a hot cooling system, or perform brake, steering or airbag repairs without proper training and equipment.
