W124 Society India

Privacy by design

A draft policy framework for the proposed community. It is not legal advice and should be reviewed before a real service collects personal data.

Current prototype

This static college-project prototype does not have a backend and does not transmit or persist form submissions. Avoid entering personal, contact or vehicle-identifying information into demonstration forms.

If the project becomes a real community

  • Collect only data needed for a clearly stated purpose.
  • Obtain clear consent for public profiles and contributed photographs.
  • Provide a contact and removal/correction process.
  • Keep registration numbers, VINs, home addresses and personal contact details private by default.
  • Document storage, access controls, retention periods and third-party services.
  • Obtain appropriate legal review and comply with applicable Indian privacy and data-protection requirements.

This draft must be updated to reflect the actual implementation before any real data collection begins.
