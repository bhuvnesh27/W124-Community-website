Knowledge with respect

A proposed code for a future enthusiast community.

Be useful, accurate and respectful

  • Distinguish personal experience, opinion and verified technical facts.
  • Include model, engine and production details where known, without publishing sensitive identifiers.
  • Link to sources and correct mistakes constructively.
  • Do not post private contact details, registration numbers, VINs or another person's images without authorisation.
  • Disclose commercial relationships and avoid undisclosed paid endorsements.
  • No harassment, spam, scams, illegal parts trading or unsafe repair encouragement.
  • Respect Team-BHP and other communities' rules; do not spam them with promotional links.

Moderation and corrections

A real community should provide a report/correction route, remove unsafe or privacy-invasive material, and maintain a transparent correction history. This prototype does not yet include moderation tools.
