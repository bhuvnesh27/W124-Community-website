A home for the 124

Not just a car. A long-running story.

The W124 has a global heritage, but Indian ownership brings its own questions: finding a well-kept example, understanding market-specific details, sourcing compatible parts and planning maintenance in local conditions.

This project brings heritage, technical learning, owner-story formats and source-led discovery into one organised destination — without pretending a live club or verified owner network already exists.
