W124 Society India

Editorial standards matter

How this student concept separates historical context, individual forum experiences, verified technical facts and prototype functionality.

Project purpose

W124 Society India is a non-commercial college project exploring how a specialist enthusiast platform could organise heritage, ownership learning and community contributions. It is not a registered club or an official Mercedes-Benz platform.

Verification labels

  • Primary-source checked: confirmed against relevant official or technical documentation.
  • Owner-reported: an individual experience attributed to its source.
  • Needs verification: a lead that should not be presented as fact yet.
  • Demonstration: fictional or illustrative content used to show the proposed interface.

Image manifest

Local assets: assets/w124-hero.png, assets/w124-street.jpg, assets/w124-workshop.jpg, assets/w124-gathering.jpg. All are AI-generated illustrations created for this project; they are not photos of actual cars and should not be presented as owner-submitted imagery.

Known limitations

  • No backend, authentication, permanent submissions, live discussion forum or image upload is implemented.
  • Registry cards are clearly labelled fictional demonstration records.
  • No verified specialist listings, prices or event dates are presented.
  • Detailed engine-specific technical specifications and repair procedures require further source work.
  • External links are provided for research and belong to their respective publishers.
